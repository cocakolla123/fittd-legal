fittd Community Guidelines

Last updated October 27, 2026 · Privacy Policy · Terms of Use · Community Guidelines · Support

fittd works because it's a safe place to show up as you are. Rate the fit, never the person.

Rate the fit, never the person

  • Votes and comments are about the clothes: the styling, the colors, the thrift find.
  • No comments on bodies, weight, faces, skin or anything about how someone looks. Not even "compliments".

Your photos

  • Your first photo is the fit pic: you, your whole outfit, shown clearly. Covering or hiding your face is fine.
  • The other photos can be anything: close-ups, friends, places, an aesthetic blur. Everything is taken in the fittd camera.
  • No ads or promo: no codes, "shop now", "DM to buy", QR codes or flyers.
  • Don't post screenshots, photos from your camera roll, or other people without their OK.

Keep it clothed

  • No nudity, underwear-only or sexual content. Swimwear is fine in a real outfit context.
  • Posts that break this are removed, and the account can be banned on the first offense.

Be decent

  • No bullying, harassment, hate, threats or targeting anyone.
  • No spam, ads or selling in captions and comments.

Play fair

  • Don't trade votes, run extra accounts or ask people to mass-vote. Crowns earned that way get removed.

If something's wrong

  • Tap ••• on any fit, comment or profile to report it. It's hidden from you right away.
  • When a fit gets 3 reports, it's hidden for everyone until we review it.
  • Block anyone, any time. They won't know.
  • If someone is in danger, contact local emergency services first. Then tell us at sai@fittd.net.
