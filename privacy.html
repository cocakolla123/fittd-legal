fittd Privacy Policy

Last updated October 27, 2026 · Privacy Policy · Terms of Use

What we collect

  • Account: your email (and your school email or school account, if you add your school), name, username, school, bio, social links and the crown lane you compete in. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we get your name and email from them.
  • Content: the fits you post, their captions, the pieces you tag, your comments, votes and saves.
  • Birthdate: entered once when you sign up. It's stored privately, never shown to anyone, and used only for the age rules: the 13+ minimum and the protections for members under 18.
  • Activity: who you friend, block and report, which school you ask fittd to open, which fits you've seen (kept 30 days, to rank feeds), and basic app events (like opening the app, posting or voting) to keep fittd working and fair and to see which features people use and come back for. App events carry your account ID, app version and iOS version, never your photos, captions, comments, name or email. Each fit also gets an automated style rating that helps rank feeds; it's never shown.
  • Device: a push notification token, if you turn notifications on.
  • We don't collect your precise location, contacts, camera roll or ad identifiers. We use your device's time zone to place you in a regional group if you don't add a school.

How it's used

  • To run fittd: showing fits, counting votes, deciding crowns, sending the notifications you've turned on.
  • To keep people safe: automated photo moderation, reviewing reports, and stopping spam and vote manipulation.
  • To pay winners: tracking Shop the fit clicks and the earnings that come from them.

Who sees what

  • You choose who sees your fits: Friends, Campus or Everyone. It applies to feeds, your profile, crowns and comments. With Friends, people who aren't your friends can still find your name and username, but not your fits, bio or links.
  • If you're under 18, your fits stay with your friends and your school, and you're kept off Global and leaderboards (see the Terms).
  • Turn off Show my school and people at other schools won't see where you go.
  • Your saves are private. Vote counts stay hidden from everyone but you until the day ends.
  • Every fit and comment is checked by AI before anyone sees it. OpenAI checks your photos, captions, tagged brands and comments for unsafe content. Anthropic (Claude) gets small copies of your photos to check that each one shows one person, that it's you in every photo and that it's one outfit, and to rate the outfit for ranking. For winning fits, Anthropic also gets the first photo and your tagged pieces to find shoppable pieces. Neither gets your name, email or username. Under their API terms they process this only for us and don't train on it; OpenAI may keep it up to 30 days to monitor abuse.
  • We also use Supabase (hosting and database), PostHog (app event analytics, with location lookup turned off), Resend (sending sign-in and school codes), Apple (push notifications), and Apple, Google or Microsoft when you sign in or verify your school with them.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal data.
  • We don't show third-party ads or share your data with advertisers.
  • When you open a shop link, the affiliate network and retailer see the visit like any website visit (your IP address and browser), not your fittd account.

Keeping and deleting

  • Your data stays while your account is open.
  • Delete your account in Settings and your profile, fits, journal, comments and votes are deleted. Copies in backups and service logs are removed on their normal schedule.
  • We may keep limited records longer when the law requires it (for example, content flagged as possible child exploitation, which we must preserve and report), or to handle a safety report or a payout.

Your rights

  • You can see and change your profile at any time, and download or delete your data by emailing sai@fittd.net.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have more rights, like objecting to some processing. Just ask.

Contact
