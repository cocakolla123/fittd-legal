fittd Privacy Policy

Last updated November 17, 2026 · Privacy Policy · Terms of Use

What we collect, why, who gets it and what you control. Short version: we keep what fittd needs to work and to keep people safe, we don't sell your data or show ads, and you can delete everything.

Who we are

  • fittd is run by ADSK Holdings (help@fittd.net). We decide how your data is used (we're the "business" or "controller").
  • Privacy questions and requests: help@fittd.net.

What we collect

  • Account: your email (or, if you sign in with Apple or Google, the name and email they give us; Apple may give us a private relay address), name, username, bio, the social handles you add, the gender you choose (Woman or Man, used only to sort your fits into the Women and Men leaderboard filters; it's never shown on your profile and isn't a separate crown) and your favorite styles.
  • School (optional): your school, and your school (or school district) email or school account (Google or Microsoft) to verify it. We keep which mailbox verified, so it can verify only one account.
  • Birthdate: entered once at sign-up. It's stored privately, never shown to anyone, and used only for the age rules: the 13+ minimum and the protections for members under 18.
  • Time zone: from your phone, to place you in a regional group if you don't add a school, and to work out your day and your age on the right date.
  • Content: the fits and weekly theme entries you post (1 to 5 photos each) and the song on each, your profile photo, captions, the pieces and brands you tag, the pieces found in your fits and photos of pieces you add to your wardrobe, styles, comments, votes, theme votes, saves and boards.
  • People: who you friend, invite, nudge, block and report, and anything you write in a report.
  • Contacts (only if you tap Sync contacts and allow it): your phone scrambles each email and phone number in your contacts into a one-way code (a SHA-256 hash) and sends only those codes, to check which belong to fittd members. We don't keep the codes or your contacts, only a count of how many were checked (for a daily limit). Names and numbers stay on your phone, and the invite list is built there. You can turn this off anytime in iOS Settings, and turn off Let contacts find me in fittd's Settings so others can't find you this way. If your fits are set to Friends, a contact is matched with you only if you already have a friend in common.
  • Activity: which fits you've seen (kept 30 days, to rank feeds), streaks and crowns, when you usually post and how often you've posted lately (to time reminders), and basic app events (like opening the app, posting or voting) to keep fittd working and fair and to see which features people use. App events carry your account ID, app version and iOS version, never your photos, captions, comments, name or email.
  • Crash and performance diagnostics: if fittd crashes, freezes or overuses battery or storage, iOS gives us a technical report (app and iOS version, phone model, the screen you were on and where in our code it happened), tied to your account ID, which we use only to fix bugs. It never includes your photos, captions, comments, name or email.
  • Style rating: each fit gets an automated rating of the outfit that helps rank feeds (and, for a weekly theme entry, how well it fits the theme). It's never shown.
  • Referrals and gift cards: whose invite link you joined with, who joined with yours and whether their fits counted, the country you confirm for gift cards and when you set it, the cards you earned and when we sent them (and the email we sent them to), and flags from our checks for fake referrals.
  • Support: what you send us by email or through the support form on fittd.net (your email, your username if you give it, and your message). The form keeps a scrambled (hashed) form of your IP address to limit how often it can be used.
  • Agreements: which version of the Terms and this policy you agreed to and when, when you agreed to the AI checks, and, if you're under 18, that a parent or guardian agreed too.
  • Device and network: a push notification token if you turn notifications on, and your IP address, which every app request carries. We use IP addresses only for security and rate limits, and our analytics location lookup is turned off.

What we don't collect

  • No precise location, camera roll or advertising identifier, and no copy of your contacts (see Contacts above). fittd only adds to your photo library when you tap Save Image.
  • No photo metadata: photos are re-encoded from their pixels on your phone and again on our servers, so location, device and time data aren't kept.
  • No face templates. Our AI checks compare the people in your other photos with the person in photo 1, but we don't create or keep faceprints or any other biometric identifier. We store only the outcome (posted, or why it was turned down).
  • Face blur finds faces on your phone only; nothing about a face is sent or kept. The check that keeps photos of people out of your wardrobe (when you add a piece with a photo) runs on your phone only too.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

How we use it

  • To run fittd: accounts and sign-in, showing fits to the audience you chose, counting votes, deciding crowns, comments, friends, boards and the notifications you've turned on.
  • To keep people safe: automated moderation, the age rules and teen protections, reviewing reports, stopping spam, fake accounts and vote manipulation, and meeting legal duties (like reporting child sexual exploitation to NCMEC).
  • To rank feeds: the fits you've seen and each fit's style rating.
  • To time reminders: when you usually post and how often you've posted lately decide whether and when a reminder goes out (only the kinds you've turned on).
  • To run referral gift cards: counting referrals, stopping fake ones, applying the lifetime limit and the country rules, and sending cards.
  • To improve fittd: app events show which features people use.
  • To train fittd's own AI (only with your yes, 18+): see "Help train the algorithm" below.
  • We don't use your data for advertising, and our AI providers don't train on it.

Help train the algorithm

  • If you're 18 or older, we ask once at sign-up whether you want to help train fittd's own AI. Saying no never limits fittd. If you say no, we ask once more about a month later, and you can change your answer anytime in Settings → Privacy.
  • If you say yes, we use the fits you post (with their ratings and tagged pieces) and every shot from each batch you shoot, including shots you don't post. Shots are sent only on Wi-Fi.
  • Every shared shot is checked first: anything unsafe (nudity, sexual content, violence, self-harm) is deleted right away and never kept. Photos with other people in them are left out of training.
  • Only fittd uses them to train its own models. We never sell your photos and never share them with anyone else to train their AI.
  • We never use photos from anyone under 18, from high school campuses, or from anyone reported as a minor, whatever their birthday says.
  • Turn it off and choose Delete what I've shared, or delete your account, and your photos are removed from our training data. Models trained before that are retrained without them on their next training run.

AI checks

  • Every fit and comment is checked by AI before anyone sees it, and you agree to this at sign-up. Posting isn't possible without these checks.
  • OpenAI checks your photos, captions, tagged brands, comments, SoundCloud song titles and any shots you share to train fittd's AI for unsafe content. OpenAI may keep this for up to 30 days to monitor abuse.
  • Anthropic (Claude) gets small copies of your photos to check that photo 1 shows one person in one outfit and that the person from photo 1 wears the same outfit in your other photos, to rate the outfit for ranking, and, for a weekly theme entry, to check it fits the theme. It only compares the people in your photos with photo 1; it never identifies anyone.
  • Your wardrobe: when it's on (it is unless you turn it off in Settings → Wardrobe), Anthropic (Claude) also gets small copies of the photos of each fit you choose to add (Add to your wardrobe is off on every post until you turn it on, and you can add an older fit from your journal), with the names and short descriptions of the pieces already in your wardrobe, to find the clothes and accessories you're wearing; to tell a repeat from a lookalike, it also compares a new piece's crop with the crop of the piece it seems to match. We crop each piece from your photo and keep it in your wardrobe, on your profile next to your journal; pieces worn near your face (hats, glasses, jewelry) are never cropped. For each new piece, OpenAI's image model also makes a product-style picture of just that piece (front, and back when it looks different) from its crop of your fit photo, marked as an AI image; it goes wherever the rest of your wardrobe goes, it's deleted with the piece, and you can switch back to the crop. OpenAI may keep these crops for up to 30 days to monitor abuse. A piece the AI isn't sure about waits for you to confirm, fix or remove it. You can also add a piece with your own photo: your phone lifts the piece out of the background, refuses photos with people in them, and the photo is checked for unsafe content like your other photos. Only you see your wardrobe unless you share it: tap its lock to pick Friends, Campus or Everyone (under 18: Friends or Campus). They see your pieces (names, brands, colors, descriptions) and their pictures, never how often or when you wore them; once you share it, OpenAI checks those words for unsafe content, like a caption, and other people only see the ones that pass; a picture cut from a fit only shows to people who can see that fit, and under each piece they only see the fits they could already see. A wardrobe shared with Everyone by someone 18 or older also has a public link (fittd.net/@handle/wardrobe) whose preview shows its pieces' names and its product photos and the photos you added, never a crop of a fit; set it back to Only me and within a few minutes the link shows nothing. It describes the items only, never you. When you delete a fit, or it's taken down, the crops cut from it are deleted too. Turning it off deletes your wardrobe, including the photos you added, and stops new fits being read; you can also remove a single piece.
  • Auto-fill (only when you tap it while posting): Anthropic (Claude) gets your photo to suggest tags for your pieces. Nothing is stored.
  • Neither gets your name, email or username. Under their API terms they process this only for us and don't train on it.

Who sees what

  • Each fit goes to the audience you pick when you post it: Friends, Campus or Everyone (Everyone also shows on Campus and to your friends). That covers feeds, crowns and comments.
  • Your journal (your fits on your profile, day by day) is for Only me, Friends, Campus or Everyone, set in Settings. With Only me, nobody else sees your past fits, friends included, and your crowns, Sleeper Picks and leaderboard spots show your profile photo instead of the fit; today's fit still shows to its audience while it's open for votes, and a weekly theme entry while its week is open. With a Friends-only journal, a fit you posted to Campus or Everyone still shows to that audience where fittd shows fits anyway: while it's open for votes, as a crown or Sleeper Pick, as a weekly theme entry while its week is open, and as your best fit (this week, this month or ever) on the Campus and Global leaderboards. You can archive any of your fits: only you see it until you bring it back. An archived fit leaves feeds, the leaderboard, other people's saves and boards; one archived before its crown is decided can't win it, and a crown it already won stays, with your profile photo in place of the fit. In a fit's first hour you can change its caption and song (checked again, as when you post) and share it with more people; you can make it more private anytime.
  • If your fits are set to Friends, people who aren't your friends can still find your name, username and profile photo, but not your bio or links.
  • A fit you share with Everyone while you're 18 or older can also be seen by anyone with its link, on fittd.net and in link previews (the photo, your name and username, and the caption). Friends and Campus fits, and fits posted while under 18, get fittd's plain card there instead.
  • If you're under 18, only your friends can comment on your fits, and we don't suggest you to adults you don't know (see the Terms).
  • Turn off Show my school and people at other schools won't see where you go.
  • Vote counts on a fit stay hidden from everyone but you until its 24 hours are up, and we never show who voted. The Friends leaderboard is live: your friends see running totals of the votes your fits get from friends, including fits still open. The gender you choose (Woman or Man) is used only to sort your fits into the Women and Men leaderboard filters. It's never shown on your profile.
  • Your saves are private. Reports are anonymous: we never tell anyone who reported them.

Who we share it with

  • Other members, as described above.
  • Service providers who run parts of fittd for us, only for that purpose: Supabase (database, storage, sign-in and server functions; United States), Cloudflare (photo storage, the fittd.net link pages and previews, and the bot check before we email a sign-in code), OpenAI and Anthropic (the AI checks above), PostHog (app event analytics), Resend (sending sign-in, school and alert emails), Apple (Sign in with Apple and push notifications), Google (Sign in with Google, school verification and our help@fittd.net inbox) and Microsoft (school verification). Each is bound by contract to protect your data at least as well as this policy does and to use it only to provide their service to us.
  • Songs (from SoundCloud only): playing a song loads SoundCloud's player from SoundCloud, which sees it like any website visit. When you add a SoundCloud link, our server looks up the track's public info on SoundCloud and checks its title and description with OpenAI's moderation. None of these carry your fittd account.
  • Gift cards: we buy Amazon.com gift cards and email them to your account's email. If a card is delivered through Amazon's own email delivery, Amazon receives the address it's sent to.
  • Sharing: when you share your fit to Instagram, Snapchat or TikTok, we hand that app the image (and a link back to fittd). Snapchat's and TikTok's sharing kits are built into fittd and may send those companies technical details about the share. What happens next is up to their privacy policies.
  • The law: we share data when the law requires it (for example, a valid subpoena or court order), to report child sexual exploitation to NCMEC as US law requires, or when we believe someone is in danger.
  • If fittd is sold or merges with another company, your data would go with it, under this policy.
  • Anyone else, only with your permission.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal data, and we don't share it for targeted ("cross-context behavioral") advertising. We haven't done either in the past 12 months.
  • We don't show third-party ads or share your data with advertisers.

Members under 18, and under 13

  • fittd is for people 13 and older. Members under 18 get extra protections (see the Terms), and we never show anyone's age or birthday.
  • We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If a birthday shows someone is under 13, sign-up stops and no profile is created. To stop the same email from signing up again with a different birthday, we keep only a one-way hash of that email (not the address) for up to 1 year, and the unfinished account is deleted within a day.
  • Fits posted while a member is under 18 never show in public link previews, never go into training, and members under 18 can't earn referral gift cards.
  • If you believe a child under 13 is using fittd, email help@fittd.net and we'll delete the account.

How long we keep it

  • Your account, content, settings and agreement records: while your account is open.
  • Fits you've seen: 30 days.
  • Security and rate-limit logs: 2 to 30 days. Sent and queued notifications: 14 days. Nudges: 14 days. Crash reports: 90 days.
  • Referral and gift card records: while your account is open. After you delete it, we keep a scrambled (hashed) copy of your email with the number of cards it earned and its last gift card country, so the lifetime limit and the 30-day country rule still apply if you sign up again, plus a log of each card (card number, country and amount, without your email) for our records. If someone you invited deletes their account, the credit you earned for them stays.
  • Support form messages: kept so we can answer and keep a record of what was asked. Ask and we'll delete yours, unless we need it for a safety or legal reason.
  • Under-13 sign-up refusals: a hash of the email for up to 1 year.
  • Moderation records (each check's result, never the photo itself) and reports: 2 years, because they can be evidence in a safety case. They stay after an account is deleted.
  • Content flagged as possible child sexual abuse material: preserved with restricted access for at least 1 year, as federal law requires (18 U.S.C. 2258A), and as long as law enforcement asks.
  • Backups: up to 7 days, then they're overwritten.

Deleting your account

  • Settings → Account → Delete account deletes your profile, fits and every photo in them, shots you shared to train fittd's AI, profile photo, journal, crowns and streaks, comments, votes, saves, boards, friends, closet, wardrobe, notifications, agreement records and settings right away. You drop off leaderboards and past crowns.
  • Kept after deletion: moderation records (without your account), reports about your account (without your profile), reports you made (without your name), the gift card records described above (a hashed email, never the address) and anything we must preserve by law (see above). Your username stays reserved for 60 days so nobody can pretend to be you, then it's forgotten. Copies in backups are removed on their normal schedule.
  • App events in our analytics tool are tied only to a random account ID, not your name or email. Deleting your account also deletes them there, and you can ask us to.
  • You can also delete any single comment, and any of your fits (today's, older ones and weekly theme entries) unless it's under review, a moderator removed it, or we have to keep it by law.

Your choices and rights

  • In the app: change your profile, who sees your fits, who sees your journal, your wardrobe (on or off, who sees it, and remove pieces), who sees each fit (and its caption and song in its first hour), archive a fit, who can send you friend requests, Show my school, Let contacts find me, Share app usage (turn off app events), Help train the algorithm (18+), notifications and reminders; block people; delete comments and fits; delete your account.
  • Notifications are off until you allow them, and you can turn them off any time in fittd or in iOS Settings.
  • Depending on where you live (for example California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut and other US states), you have the right to know what we collect and why, to get a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, and to opt out of sales, sharing for ads and profiling (we do none of these). We give these rights to everyone, wherever you live, and we won't treat you differently for using them.
  • To make a request, use Settings → Support → Request your data, or email help@fittd.net from the email on your account (or tell us your username). We'll confirm it's you, then answer within 45 days. You can use an authorized agent, and you can appeal a decision by replying to our answer.
  • California residents under 18 can remove content they posted (delete it in the app, or ask us). We use sensitive information like your birthdate only to run fittd and keep people safe, as California law permits.

If you're in the EEA, UK or Switzerland

  • We use your data because it's needed to provide fittd under the Terms (your account, posting, votes, crowns, friends, gift cards you earn); for our legitimate interests in keeping fittd safe, fair and working (moderation and the AI checks, stopping fraud and abuse, feed ranking, app events and crash reports); to meet legal obligations; and with your consent (contacts matching, notifications and Help train the algorithm). You can take back consent anytime in Settings; that doesn't affect what we did before.
  • On top of the rights above, you can object to what we do based on legitimate interests, ask us to restrict it, and get your data in a portable format. You can complain to your local data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO).
  • Your data is transferred to the United States. Where the law requires it, we rely on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses (with the UK addendum) in our contracts with service providers.
  • If you're younger than the age at which you can agree to this yourself where you live (13 to 16 in the EU, 13 in the UK), a parent or guardian must agree for you.

Security

  • Data is encrypted in transit, access is limited by row-level rules on every table, and only a few people can reach production data. No system is perfect; if a breach affects you, we'll tell you as the law requires.

Where your data is

  • fittd is run from the United States and your data is stored and processed mainly in the United States (Cloudflare may serve photos and pages from servers nearer to you). If you use fittd from another country, your data is transferred to the United States.

Changes

  • If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect, ask you to agree to the new version, and update the effective date above.
