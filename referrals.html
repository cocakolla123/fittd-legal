Referral Gift Cards

Effective November 14, 2026 · Terms of Use · Privacy Policy

Invite 10 friends who join and post a fit. Earn a $20 gift card. These rules are part of the fittd Terms of Use.

Who can earn

  • You must be 18 or older. Members in any country can earn, except countries gift cards can't legally be sent to (currently Cuba, Iran, North Korea and Syria). You confirm your country in the app; we don't go by your phone's region.
  • Members under 18 can invite friends and can be someone else's referral, but can't earn gift cards.

What counts as a referral

  • Someone new to fittd who uses your invite link within 7 days of creating their account. Only the first invite link they use counts, and each person counts once.
  • They post an outfit that passes moderation and stays posted and approved for 72 hours in a row, counted on our servers from the moment it's approved. Friends, Campus or Everyone all count.
  • Your own account, and fake, duplicate, automated, bought or repeat accounts (including someone deleting their account and signing up again) don't count.

Deleting outfits

  • You, and the people you invite, can always delete outfits and accounts.
  • An outfit deleted, or removed for breaking the rules, before its 72 hours are up doesn't count, but another outfit can.
  • Once a referral has counted, it stays counted even if the outfit or the account is deleted later, unless it's found to break these rules.

The reward

  • Every 10 referrals that count earn one $20 USD Amazon.com gift card. It's the same card in every country.
  • Amazon.com gift cards can be used on Amazon.com only, not on other Amazon stores (such as Amazon.in or Amazon.co.uk). Amazon.com ships to many countries, but not every product ships everywhere.

Lifetime limit

  • Each eligible member may earn a maximum of 10 referral rewards, totaling $200 USD. After reaching this lifetime limit, additional referrals will not earn further gift-card rewards.
  • Changing your country, username, email or phone doesn't reset the limit. You can keep inviting friends after reaching it.

Delivery

  • The fittd team emails each gift card to your account's email address within 7 days of it being earned, or of you confirming your country if that's later.

Fair play

  • We may review referrals and hold a reward while we check. We may remove referrals and cancel unsent rewards that break these rules. Sharing a home, school or network with someone isn't a problem by itself.
  • We may change or end this offer. Rewards earned before a change are still sent.

Amazon is not a sponsor of this offer. Apple is not a sponsor of, and is not involved in, this offer.
