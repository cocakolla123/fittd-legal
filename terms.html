fittd Terms of Use

Last updated October 24, 2026 · Privacy Policy · Terms of Use

Who can use fittd

  • You must be 13 or older.
  • You must be a current student with a working email address at a supported school. We verify it with a one-time code when you sign in.
  • One account per person. Don't sign up for someone else or pretend to be anyone you're not.

If you're under 18

  • Only your friends and people verified at your school see your fits. Without a school, only your friends do. Everyone isn't available until you turn 18; then you can turn it on in Settings.
  • You're not on Global, in Global crowns or on any leaderboard. You can still look at all of them, and you can still win Campus and Friends crowns.
  • People you aren't friends with can't find you in search or open your profile, unless they're also under 18 at your school. Adults can't send you friend requests, but you can send them one, and anyone you give your invite link to can add you.
  • Only your friends can comment on your fits.
  • You can report or block anyone, any time. We never show your age or birthday to anyone.

Your account

  • Keep your school email secure; anyone who can read it can sign in as you.
  • Your username can change once every 30 days, so people can still find you.
  • You can sign out or delete your account at any time in Settings.

Your fits and your content

  • You own the photos, captions and comments you post.
  • You give fittd a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license to host, show, resize and share your content inside the app, and to feature winning fits on fittd's own social accounts. This license ends when you delete the content or your account, except for copies other people already shared or that we must keep by law.
  • Only post photos you took of yourself, in the in-app camera. Don't post anyone else.

Crowns and voting

  • Each day runs until local midnight at your school. Crowns are decided after midnight and revealed in the morning.
  • Voting is about the outfit. Gaming the vote (fake accounts, vote trading rings, automation) can get crowns removed and accounts banned.
  • We may adjust or withhold a crown if a fit breaks these terms or the Community Guidelines.

Safety and moderation

  • Every photo is checked by automated moderation before it's shown, and anything can be reported.
  • When a fit gets 3 reports from different people, it's hidden automatically until a person reviews it.
  • We review reports within 24 hours and remove content or accounts that break the rules. Zero tolerance for nudity, sexual content, harassment, hate and threats.
  • You can block anyone. Blocking hides each of you from the other everywhere in the app.

Shop the fit and affiliate links

  • Winning fits on Campus and Global can be decoded into pieces you can shop. Pieces tagged by the creator are marked Exact; AI matches are marked Similar and may not be the same item.
  • Shop links are affiliate links. fittd may earn a commission on purchases, and the winner gets a share of it.
  • Earnings show as pending until the retailer confirms the sale. Payouts depend on retailers' rules and may be reversed if an order is returned or cancelled.
