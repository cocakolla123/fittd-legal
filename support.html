Help with fittd.

Get in touch

  • Email <a href="mailto:sai@fittd.net">sai@fittd.net</a>. We reply within 2 business days, and to safety reports within 24 hours.
  • If someone is in danger, contact local emergency services first.

Signing in

  • fittd signs you in with a one-time code sent to your email. Check spam or promotions if it doesn't arrive, and ask for a new one after 60 seconds.
  • Signed up with Apple or Google? Use the same button again to get back into the same account.

Posting

  • You get one fit a day, and the day ends at local midnight. Your first photo has to show you and your whole outfit; the other photos can be anything.
  • If a photo is turned down for being unclear or not showing the whole fit, retakes don't count against your daily limit.

Reporting and blocking

  • Tap ••• on any fit, comment or profile to report or block. A report hides it from you right away, and we review every report.
  • See the <a href="guidelines.html">Community Guidelines</a> for what's allowed.

Your account and data

  • Delete your account any time in Settings → Delete account. Your profile, fits, journal, comments and votes are deleted.
  • To download your data or ask a privacy question, email <a href="mailto:sai@fittd.net">sai@fittd.net</a>. See the <a href="privacy.html">Privacy Policy</a>.
