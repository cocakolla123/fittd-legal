fittd Privacy Policy

Last updated October 24, 2026 · Privacy Policy · Terms of Use

What we collect

  • Account: your email (and your school email or school account, if you add your school), name, username, school, bio, social links and the crown lane you compete in. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we get your name and email from them.
  • Content: the fits you post, their captions, the pieces you tag, your comments, votes and saves.
  • Birthdate: entered once when you sign up. It's stored privately, never shown to anyone, and used only for the age rules: the 13+ minimum and the protections for members under 18.
  • Activity: who you friend, block and report, which school you ask fittd to open, and basic app events (like opening the app or posting) to keep fittd working and fair.
  • Device: a push notification token, if you turn notifications on.
  • We don't collect your location, contacts, camera roll or ad identifiers.

How it's used

  • To run fittd: showing fits, counting votes, deciding crowns, sending the notifications you've turned on.
  • To keep people safe: automated photo moderation, reviewing reports, and stopping spam and vote manipulation.
  • To pay winners: tracking Shop the fit clicks and the earnings that come from them.

Who sees what

  • You choose who sees your fits: Friends, Campus or Everyone. It applies to feeds, your profile, crowns and comments.
  • If you're under 18, your fits stay with your friends and your school, and you're kept off Global and leaderboards (see the Terms).
  • Turn off Show my school and people at other schools won't see where you go.
  • Your saves are private. Vote counts stay hidden from everyone but you until the day ends.
  • Every fit you post is checked by AI before anyone sees it: OpenAI checks photos and captions for unsafe content, and Anthropic (Claude) checks that each photo shows one person, that it's you in every photo, and that it's one outfit, and rates the outfit to help rank feeds. Anthropic also analyzes winning fits to find shoppable pieces. They get the photos, the caption and the brands you tag, process them only to do these checks for us, and don't use them to train their models.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal data.
  • We don't show third-party ads or share your data with advertisers.
  • Affiliate retailers learn that someone clicked a link, not who you are.

Keeping and deleting

  • Your data stays while your account is open.
  • Delete your account in Settings and your profile, fits, journal, comments and votes are deleted. Backups roll off within 30 days.
  • We may keep limited records longer when the law requires it, or to handle a safety report or a payout.

Your rights

  • You can see and change your profile at any time, and download or delete your data by emailing sai@fittd.net.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have more rights, like objecting to some processing. Just ask.

Contact

  • Privacy questions: sai@fittd.net.
