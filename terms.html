Crowns, votes and themes

  • Each day runs until 12am Eastern time, for everyone. You can post one fit a day, and one entry a week for the weekly theme. Each fit is open to votes for 24 hours from when it's posted. Fit of the Day is decided at 12am Eastern from the votes cast by then, among fits whose 24 hours overlap the day just ended, and a fit can win it only once. Weekly theme crowns are decided after the week ends.
  • Crowns are recognition only. They have no monetary value, can't be bought, sold or transferred, and don't come with a prize. Posting and voting are free: no purchase or payment is ever needed to take part or to win.
  • Crowns are decided by votes, counted under fittd's rules (for example, minimum numbers of entrants; Campus counts votes from people at the poster's school, Friends counts votes from the poster's friends, and Global counts every vote). Each feed has one Fit of the Day. Your Women or Men pick only sets the leaderboard filter your fits show under; it isn't a separate crown. We may change these rules, and our decision on a crown is final.
  • Vote counts on a fit stay hidden from everyone but the person who posted it until its 24 hours are up, and we never show who voted. Leaderboards are live and count fits that are still open, so they show running totals: Friends adds up the votes each friend's fits get from their friends, and Campus and Global add up each member's votes. Each shows a member's best fit there.
  • fittd decides the order of fits in your feeds. It looks at things like how many of the people who saw a fit voted for it, whether you've seen it already, and an automated style score from our photo check that gives new fits a head start until people vote. We may change how this works. Fit of the Day and theme crowns are decided by votes alone; the Sleeper Pick also uses the style score.
  • Voting is about the outfit. We can withhold, remove or correct a crown if there was an error or if a fit or vote broke these terms or the Community Guidelines.
  • If we ever offer a real prize, it will come with its own official rules, and those rules will apply.
  • Apple isn't a sponsor of crowns, votes or themes and isn't involved with them in any way.
